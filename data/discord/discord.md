## Overview

Discord is a group chat app popular with gamers and online communities, but it can also be used as an internal communications tool. It offers nonprofit teams a more casual way to connect, ideal for hopping in and out of voice channels or text conversations.

## Key Features

- Group chats and channels
- Text and voice chat
- Emojis, stickers, and soundboard effects
- Streaming
- Online statuses

## Pricing

Discord is free to use, but you can pay for upgrades to enhance your experience.

## Integrations

You can use Zapier to connect Discord to a range of apps such as Google Calendar, Trello, and Notion.

## Use Cases

Casual internal team communication for nonprofits that want a streamlined Slack alternative.
