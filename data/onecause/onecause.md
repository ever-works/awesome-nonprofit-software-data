## Overview

OneCause is a fundraising platform that specializes in events and auctions.

## Features

- Event ticketing and registration
- QR code check-ins
- Auction item gallery
- Competitive bidding
- Scorecards and slideshows
- Real-time analytics

## Pricing

OneCause's pricing is available on a per-quote basis, with different plans based on your specific goals and needs.

## Integrations

- DonorDock
- Double the Donation
- Virtuous

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS platform.
