## Overview

Givebutter Plus enhances Givebutter's fundraising platform with productivity features, making it easier to manage all fundraising efforts in one place.

## Key Features

- Pre-built and custom workflows
- Task management
- Customization with tags, fields, and notes
- File uploads
- Data visualizations
- Advanced drag-and-drop email editor
- Built-in text messaging for reminders, appeals, and real-time updates

## Pricing

Givebutter's core fundraising features are free to use. Givebutter Plus starts at $29 per month and scales depending on the number of contacts.

## Integrations

Givebutter integrates with hundreds of other tools through its integrations library.

## Use Cases

Managing fundraising efforts, task tracking, donor insights, email campaigns, and text messaging for reminders and appeals.
