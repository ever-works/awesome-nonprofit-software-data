## Overview

Xero is a cloud-based accounting platform designed to serve nonprofits and charities alongside small to medium enterprises. It provides real-time financial dashboards showing donations, grants, and expenses, built-in payroll functionality, and a wide ecosystem of integrations to tie fundraising, volunteer, and CRM systems into one unified finance view.

## Key Features

- Real-time financial dashboard showing donations, grants, and expenses
- Built-in payroll functionality: automatic wage calculations, VAT/deductions, payslips, staff self-service
- Strong integrations: more than 1,000 third-party apps for fundraising, CRM, and volunteer systems

## Pricing

Three pricing tiers: $29/month (Starter), $46/month (Standard), and $69/month (Premium). A discount for charity status is available.

## Integrations

More than 1,000 third-party apps for fundraising, CRM, and volunteer systems.

## Use Cases

Simplifies accounting and payroll for organizations that may not have large finance teams, with grant tracking, restricted funds, and payroll compliance.
