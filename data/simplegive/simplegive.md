## Overview

SimpleGive is an easy-to-use platform for churches and non-profits to manage donations seamlessly. It offers various ways to give—online, by text, or even through a kiosk—and all options are secure and customizable to fit the organization's brand, with helpful tools for tracking and reporting donations.

## Features

- Matching Gifts
- Fund Management
- Customizable Forms
- Mobile Giving
- Donor Database
- Volunteer Management
- Event Registration
- Payment Processing
- Online Giving
- Text Giving
- Kiosk Giving
- Donation Tracking and Reporting
