## Overview

Funraise is an all-in-one growth platform built by nonprofit fundraisers to eliminate software headaches.

## Features

- Customizable fundraising sites
- Events with tabling and auctions
- Peer-to-peer campaigns
- Built-in donor CRM
- Reports and dashboards
- Time-saving automations
- High-converting donation forms

## Pricing

Free core plan; add-ons and custom plans available.

## Integrations

- Salesforce
- HubSpot
- QuickBooks
- Blackbaud Raiser's Edge
- Mailchimp
- Zapier
- DAFpay
- Kindsight

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS platform.
