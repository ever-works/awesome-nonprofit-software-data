## Overview

monday.com is a cloud-based project management software that helps streamline processes, increase productivity, automate tasks, and provide functionality for teams running fundraising campaigns.

## Key Features

- Donation pipelines
- Automated emails and texts
- Donor profiles
- Volunteer recruitment forms
- Grant management
- Task management
- End-to-end project management

## Pricing

monday.com offers a generous discount for nonprofits. Eligible organizations can use ten seats for free, with any additional seats enjoying a 70% discount off the standard plan price.

## Integrations

The platform integrates with numerous software solutions, including Givebutter's monday.com integration, which syncs donor and campaign data between your Givebutter dashboard and monday.com's fundraising boards.

## Use Cases

Planning, tracking, and analyzing fundraising campaigns; task and event management; donor and volunteer management; grant management.
