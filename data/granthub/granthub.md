## Overview

GrantHub is a dedicated grant management tool that helps nonprofits track deadlines, tasks, and relationships for every grant in one place.

## Features

- Grant deadline and task tracking
- Centralized grant and funder records
- Document storage and version history
- Activity tracking and reminders
- Reporting on submissions, awards, and success rates

## Pricing

Pricing typically starts around $95 per month, with higher-tier plans available for larger teams and more advanced needs.

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS platform.
