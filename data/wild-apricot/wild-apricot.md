## Overview

Wild Apricot is a payment processing and membership management software that allows organizations to take and track online and offline payments and manage their finances securely. It supports automatic recurring payments and multiple sales taxes.

## Target Audience

- StartUps
- SMBs
- Mid-Market
- Enterprises

## Features

- Event Registration Payments
- Refund Management
- Software Integration
- Automated Receipts
- Payment Reminders
- Email Notifications
- Secure Transactions
- Online Payments
