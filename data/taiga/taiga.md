## Overview

Taiga is an open-source project management platform for agile development teams. It supports Kanban and Scrum methodologies with backlog management, issue tracking, sprint planning, and collaboration features.

## Key Features

- Kanban and Scrum boards
- Product backlog management
- Issue tracking
- Sprint planning
- Epics and user stories
- Open source (self-hosted option available)

## Pricing

Free and open-source. Cloud and self-hosted deployment options available.
