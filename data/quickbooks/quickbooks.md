## Overview

QuickBooks is a well-known accounting software solution that many nonprofits adopt because it combines accessible financial management, cash-flow tracking, invoicing, and expense monitoring, and can scale to add payroll services as the organization grows. It is well-suited for smaller or mid-sized charities seeking professional-grade accounting without a large finance team.

## Key Features

- User-friendly interface and mobile app access
- Solid reporting tools: track cash flow, expenses by project/fund, donor/grant money
- Banking and payment system integrations for invoices, donations, and reimbursements
- Scalable payroll services

## Pricing

Regular monthly pricing in the U.S. begins at around $38/month for very basic versions. Through the nonprofit partner program (via TechSoup), eligible 501(c)(3) organizations can access substantially reduced rates, for example a one-year admin fee of ~$75 for QuickBooks Online Plus versus regular many hundreds per month.

## Integrations

Banking and payment system integrations; Givebutter Plus has a built-in QuickBooks Online integration for exporting donation data.

## Use Cases

Professional-grade accounting for smaller and mid-sized charities, cash-flow tracking, invoicing, expense monitoring, and payroll as the organization grows.
