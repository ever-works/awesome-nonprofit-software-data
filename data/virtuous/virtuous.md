## Overview

Virtuous is a comprehensive fundraising platform that empowers nonprofits to enhance donor engagement through personalized experiences. It offers tools for CRM, marketing, volunteer management, and event planning to boost generosity and streamline operations.

## Features

- Task Management
- Segmentation
- Volunteer Management
- Custom Fields
- Marketing Automation
- Donor Management
- Social Media
- Grant Management

## Target Segments

- StartUps
- SMBs
- Mid-Market
- Enterprises

## Pricing

- Standard: $199 per month (annual billing)
- Pro: $449 per month (annual billing)
- Enterprise: $849 per month

## User Sentiment

The reviewer finds Virtuous easy to learn with clear help guides, videos, and responsive support, noting a shallow learning curve. They also cite add-on feature costs as a drawback for smaller nonprofits, though integration options can offset some needs. Integration is available through API or upload.
