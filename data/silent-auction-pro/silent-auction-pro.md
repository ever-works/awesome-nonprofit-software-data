## Overview

Silent Auction Pro enables nonprofits to host impactful auctions and events, but it also offers a range of other fundraising features.

## Features

- Auction catalog
- Paddle raising
- Buy-it-now feature
- Donation tracking
- Event ticketing
- Marketing and communications

## Pricing

Silent Auction Pro's pricing starts at $749 per year for virtual, hybrid, and in-person events, or $449 per year for in-person-only features.

## Integrations

- DocHub
