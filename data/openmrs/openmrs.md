## Overview

OpenMRS is a free and open-source medical record system (EMR/EHR platform). It provides electronic medical records functionality for healthcare organizations in resource-constrained environments, particularly in developing countries.

## Key Features

- Electronic medical records
- Patient management
- Clinical workflows
- Modular architecture
- Open source (self-hosted)
