## Overview

Decidim is a free open-source participatory democracy framework for cities and organizations. It enables citizen participation, proposal creation, debate, and decision-making processes for municipalities and organizations.

## Key Features

- Participatory democracy processes
- Citizen proposals and debates
- Decision-making tools
- Meeting and assembly management
- Open source (self-hosted)
