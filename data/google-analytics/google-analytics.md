## Overview

Google Analytics is a go-to tool for analytics and SEO for nonprofits, thanks to its ease of use and familiarity. Knowing where visitors come from, how they find you, and what they do on your website allows you to engage with supporters more effectively.

## Key Features

- Traffic and visitor analysis
- Audience insights
- Visitor action data
- Marketing campaign success measurement
- E-commerce tracking

## Pricing

Google Analytics is completely free. To get started, you just need to create an account and connect it to your nonprofit website.

## Integrations

You can integrate Google Analytics with almost any website software or platform.

## Use Cases

Web analytics and SEO for nonprofits; measuring website traffic, visitor behavior, and marketing campaign success.
