## Overview

Donor Engine is a comprehensive software solution designed to streamline donor management, enhance fundraising efforts, and simplify event planning.

## Features

- Text Messaging
- Fundraising Campaigns
- Matching Gifts
- Donor Database
- Online Donations
- Event Management
- Pledge Management
- Recurring Donations
