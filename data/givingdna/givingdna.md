## Overview

GivingDNA is a wealth screening tool that nonprofits can use to gain deeper insights about their donors.

## Features

- Wealth screening
- Moves management
- Portfolio optimization
- Reporting
- Analytics
- Data visualization

## Pricing

GivingDNA's pricing is based on the number of records you have, but the price isn't listed publicly online.

## Integrations

- Salesforce
- Blackbaud NXT
- Virtuous

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS platform.
