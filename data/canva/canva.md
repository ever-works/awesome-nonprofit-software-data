## Overview

Canva is a design platform that makes graphic design accessible through an intuitive, non-technical interface. Nonprofits can customize templates to craft engaging graphics for email campaigns, social media campaigns, digital ads, and more.

## Key Features

- Built-in templates and graphic elements
- Document creation
- Presentations and whiteboards
- Photo and video editor
- AI image generator
- Brand and asset management

## Pricing

Canva operates on a freemium model with basic features available for free. Eligible nonprofits can enjoy Canva's premium features for free through Canva for Nonprofits.

## Integrations

Canva integrates with many tools to streamline creative workflows, including Givebutter's built-in Canva integration.

## Use Cases

Nonprofits use Canva to design graphics for email campaigns, social media campaigns, digital ads, presentations, and print materials.
