## Overview

Wildbook is Wild Me's first product, an open source software platform that supports wildlife researchers by allowing collaboration across the globe and automation of photo ID matching. The platform uses computer vision to identify individual animals from photographs, enabling population tracking and conservation research.

## Key Features

- Photo identification and individual animal recognition
- Global researcher collaboration tools
- Computer vision and machine learning for animal ID matching
- Population tracking and analytics
- Open source codebase
- Multi-species support (whales, sharks, giraffes, zebras, and more)

## Deployment

Cloud-based platform with open source deployment options.

## Integrations

- Wild Me computer vision APIs
- Research partner platforms
- Conservation data repositories

## Pricing

Free and open source for research and conservation use.
