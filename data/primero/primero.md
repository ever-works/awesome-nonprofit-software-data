## Overview

Primero is an open-source application designed to help child protection workers and social workers in humanitarian and development contexts manage data on vulnerable children and survivors of violence.

## Key Features

- Case management for child protection
- Data management on vulnerable children
- Incident tracking
- Open source (self-hosted)
