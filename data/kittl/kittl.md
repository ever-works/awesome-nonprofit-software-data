## Overview

Kittl is a simplified online design tool for creatives and businesses that want to quickly create graphics for online, print, and products. It is especially useful for nonprofits that want to add print-on-demand branded merch to their online store.

## Key Features

- Design templates
- Exclusive fonts
- Text effects
- Product mockups
- Image editing
- Moodboards

## Pricing

Kittl has a free plan. Paid plans cost around $18 per month to access premium content, graphics, and fonts.

## Integrations

It is not clear whether Kittl has direct integrations, but it can be used alongside tools within the design and product workflow, such as Etsy and Printify.

## Use Cases

Creating graphics for online, print, and products, including print-on-demand branded merch for nonprofit online stores.
