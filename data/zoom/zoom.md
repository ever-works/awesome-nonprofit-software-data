## Overview

Zoom is one of the most widely used webinar and virtual meeting platforms, trusted by nonprofits for donor briefings, board meetings, trainings, and large-scale virtual events.

## Features

- Webinars and meetings for small or large audiences
- Screen sharing, breakout rooms, polls, and Q&A
- Reliable video and audio quality across devices
- Recording and on-demand playback
- Familiar, low-friction join experience for attendees
- Nonprofit discounts available through Zoom for Nonprofits

## Pricing

Zoom offers nonprofit discounts through Zoom for Nonprofits, with pricing based on plan type and webinar size. Organizations must apply to confirm eligibility.

## Integrations

Zoom connects with major CRMs, calendars, and marketing tools—and works seamlessly with Givebutter livestreaming.

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS platform.
