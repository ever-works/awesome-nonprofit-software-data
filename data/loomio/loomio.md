## Overview

Loomio is free and open-source collaborative decision making and forum software. It enables teams and organizations to discuss proposals, make decisions, and keep records of discussions in a transparent way.

## Key Features

- Collaborative decision making
- Discussion forums
- Proposal and voting tools
- Decision recording
- Open source (self-hosted)
