## Overview

GoFundMe Pro is an online fundraising platform designed for nonprofits to personalize their outreach at scale.

## Features

- Donation forms
- Peer-to-peer fundraising
- Event ticketing and registration
- Auctions and mobile bidding
- Recurring giving
- Crowdfunding
- Donation websites

## Pricing

Pricing isn't available online; nonprofits request a demo for a custom quote, with packages adjusted to organization needs.

## Integrations

- DonorDock
- DonorPerfect
- Double the Donation
- Zapier (higher-tier plans)

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS platform.
