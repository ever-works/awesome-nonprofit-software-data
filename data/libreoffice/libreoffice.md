## Overview

LibreOffice is a full, open-source office suite providing word processing, spreadsheets, presentations, drawings, and database tools for nonprofit organizations as a free alternative to proprietary office software.

## Key Features

- Writer (word processing)
- Calc (spreadsheets)
- Impress (presentations)
- Draw (vector graphics)
- Base (database)
- Open source (self-hosted/desktop)
