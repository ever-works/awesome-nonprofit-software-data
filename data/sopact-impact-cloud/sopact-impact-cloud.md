## Overview

Sopact Impact Cloud (also marketed under the name Sopact Sense) is a unified impact-measurement and data-analytics platform built for nonprofits and social-impact organizations to collect, clean, analyze, and report on both quantitative and qualitative data.

## Key Features

- Built-in survey and feedback collection workflows with logic, unique links, and stakeholder-level tracking
- AI-enabled analysis of open-ended responses, PDF uploads, and mixed-methods data (qualitative + quantitative)
- Live dashboards, shareable reports, and real-time measurement of outcomes

## Pricing

Sopact does not publicly list its pricing. You will have to reach out to their team to find out what the software will cost you.

## Integrations

Connects with program data sources across many tools to synthesize impact data for funder dashboards and decision-making.

## Use Cases

Collecting, cleaning, analyzing, and reporting quantitative and qualitative program data; preparing evidence for funder dashboards and decision-making.
