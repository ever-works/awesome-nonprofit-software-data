## Overview

Reachapp is a customizable, scalable sponsorship and donor management software designed for nonprofits, built for efficient and secure management of donations and sponsorships at an affordable cost.

## Key Features

- Online Giving
- Software Integration
- Email Campaigns
- Data Export
- Donor Profiles
- Customizable Reports
- Mobile Access
- Task Management
- Donor Portals
- Mobile Apps
- Detailed Reporting

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) platform.
