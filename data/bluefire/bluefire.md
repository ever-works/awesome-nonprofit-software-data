## Overview

BlueFire is a user-friendly platform designed to facilitate secure online donations through computers, tablets, and smartphones. Established in 2008, it offers customizable giving forms, text giving, and event registration solutions for churches, ministries, and non-profits.

## Features

- Grant Management
- Social Media
- Volunteer Management
- Reporting And Analytics
- Donor Profiles
- Data Export
- Automated Thank You Letters
- Recurring Donations
- Customizable Giving Forms
- Text Giving
- Event Registration
