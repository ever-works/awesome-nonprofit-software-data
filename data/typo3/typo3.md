## Overview

TYPO3 is a fully featured and reliable, free, open-source enterprise content management system (CMS). It provides enterprise-grade content management, multi-site capabilities, workflow management, and extensibility for large nonprofit organizations.

## Key Features

- Enterprise content management
- Multi-site and multi-language support
- Workflow management
- Extensible architecture
- Open source (self-hosted)
