## Overview

eGiving.com is a secure online donation platform built for nonprofits and churches to increase donations through multiple giving channels and real-time reporting.

## Key Features

- Grant Management
- Fund Accounting
- Donor Segmentation
- Donor Database
- Mobile Giving
- Text To Give
- Volunteer Management
- Membership Management
- Customizable Interfaces
- Real-Time Reporting

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) platform accessible from any device.
