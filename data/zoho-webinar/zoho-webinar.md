## Overview

Zoho Webinar is a simple, browser-based webinar platform that makes it easy for nonprofits to host trainings, fundraising events, town halls, and educational sessions without technical setup.

## Features

- No download required – attendees join via browser across all major platforms
- Engagement tools like live chat, Q&A, and polls for interactive sessions
- Basic analytics included (attendance, engagement) even on a free plan
- Nonprofit discounts available

## Pricing

Zoho Webinar offers a free plan for smaller audiences, with paid plans starting at around $8 per month for up to 25 attendees. Higher-tier plans range from roughly $16 to $66 per month, depending on audience size and storage needs.

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS platform.
