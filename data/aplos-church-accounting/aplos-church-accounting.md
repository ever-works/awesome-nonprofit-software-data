## Overview

Aplos Church Accounting is a cloud-based nonprofit software program focused on donor management, fundraising, and church accounting needs.

## Features

- Attendance Tracking
- Member Management
- Pledge Tracking
- Bank Reconciliation
- Budgeting
- Customizable Reports
- Recurring Donations
- Volunteer Management
