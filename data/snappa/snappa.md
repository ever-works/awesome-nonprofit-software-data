## Overview

Snappa allows users to create design elements for the web in no time using templates and built-in graphics. Fundraisers can benefit from a library of premade assets to customize and share on social media quickly.

## Key Features

- Templates
- High-quality graphics and photos
- Background remover
- Image resizing

## Pricing

Snappa has a free plan limited to three downloads per month. The Pro plan is $10 per month for unlimited downloads, custom fonts, and advanced design features.

## Integrations

Snappa integrates with social media tools such as Buffer to make the design and publishing workflow simple.

## Use Cases

Fundraisers can create graphics in minutes to share on social media without worrying about design details.
