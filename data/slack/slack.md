## Overview

Slack gives nonprofit team members a simple method to stay in touch, wherever they are, helping teams avoid missing messages, opportunities, and roadblocks.

## Key Features

- Asynchronous and real-time team communication
- Open and private communication channels
- User profiles
- Tags and comment threads
- Direct messages

## Pricing

Businesses normally pay a monthly fee for Slack's Pro subscription. Eligible nonprofits can request a Slack for Charities discount that offers a free Pro upgrade for the first 250 workspace members and an 85% discount for workspaces above that size.

## Integrations

Many other software tools integrate with Slack so you can get updates and share data within the tool, including Givebutter's Slack integration via Zapier.

## Use Cases

Team coordination for in-person, hybrid, and remote nonprofit teams; coordinating programs, collaborating on grants, and keeping remote volunteers in the loop.
