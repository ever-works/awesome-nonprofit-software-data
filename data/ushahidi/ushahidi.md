## Overview

Ushahidi is an open-source geo data content management and collection system. It enables crowdsourced information collection, mapping, and visualization for crisis response, human rights monitoring, election monitoring, and community reporting.

## Key Features

- Crowdsourced data collection
- Geospatial mapping and visualization
- Crisis response and monitoring
- Mobile and web reporting
- Open source (self-hosted)
