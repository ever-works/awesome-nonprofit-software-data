## Overview

Sahana EDEN is open-source disaster management software. It provides disaster response coordination, needs assessment, and resource management for humanitarian organizations during crises and disasters.

## Key Features

- Disaster response coordination
- Needs assessment
- Resource management
- Shelter management
- Open source (self-hosted)
