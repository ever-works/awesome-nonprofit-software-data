## Overview

Abila is nonprofit management software that enhances donor engagement programs. The donor management software creates engaging membership and granting events and makes donors feel valued and special.

## Target Audience

- StartUps
- SMBs
- Mid-Market
- Enterprises

## Features

- Reporting And Analytics
- Project Accounting
- Bank Reconciliation
- Grant Management
- Revenue Management
- Expense Tracking
- General Ledger
- Multi Currency
