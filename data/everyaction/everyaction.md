## Overview

EveryAction is a comprehensive and user-friendly CRM and fundraising platform designed for non-profit organizations. It provides an all-in-one solution for non-profit organizations to manage their supporters, donations, and fundraising campaigns.

## Features

- Donor management
- Fundraising
- Advocacy
- Volunteer management
- Advanced reporting and analytics
- Marketing automation
- Robust integrations

## Pricing

- Starting price: $49/month
- Custom pricing for larger organizations

## Pros

- User-friendly interface
- Comprehensive and integrated features
- Advanced reporting and analytics capabilities
- Strong advocacy and volunteer management tools
- Wide range of integrations
- Excellent customer support

## Cons

- Expensive pricing
- Steep learning curve
- Limited customization options
- Some features may not be necessary for smaller non-profit organizations
