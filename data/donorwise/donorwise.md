## Overview

DonorWise is comprehensive donation management software developed by TntWare, designed to streamline the processing and reporting of contributions for mission organizations.

## Features

- User Permissions
- Donor Segmentation
- Matching Gifts
- Mobile Access
- Donation Management
- Pledge Management
- Payment Management
- Customizable Forms
