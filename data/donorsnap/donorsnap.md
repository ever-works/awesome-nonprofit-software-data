## Overview

DonorSnap is a cloud-based donor management and fundraising software specially designed for non-profit organizations to optimize their fundraising operations.

## Features

- Online donations forms
- Donor management
- Fundraising campaign management
- Email marketing
- Reporting and analytics
- Integration
- Mobile app
- Volunteer Management

## Pricing

- Up to 1,000 Contacts: $39 per month
- Up to 2,500 Contacts: $59 per month
- Up to 5,000 Contacts: $79 per month
- Up to 10,000 Contacts: $99 per month
- Up to 30,000 Contacts: $199 per month
