## Overview

Online Charity ERP is an online charity ERP cloud solution for non-profit organizations. It enables seamless management of donations, projects, and resources in one secure platform with intuitive accessibility from any device.

## Features

- Matching Gifts
- Mobile Giving
- Data Security
- Email Marketing
- Donation Forms
- Pledge Management
- Customizable Reports
- CRM Integration
