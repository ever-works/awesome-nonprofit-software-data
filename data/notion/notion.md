## Overview

Notion is a highly customizable workspace, collaboration, and project management tool. Nonprofit teams can use Notion to manage not only fundraising or marketing campaigns but also their overall business operations.

## Key Features

- Project management
- Task management
- Document management
- Wiki feature
- Built-in AI assistant

## Pricing

Notion is free for individuals. Plans for small teams and businesses start at around $11 monthly per user.

## Integrations

Notion has more than 100 native integrations, including tools like Canva, Google Drive, and Dropbox.

## Use Cases

Managing fundraising and marketing campaigns, business operations, documentation, and team collaboration for nonprofit teams.
