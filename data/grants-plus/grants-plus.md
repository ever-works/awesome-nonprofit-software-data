## Overview

Grants Plus is a grants platform that combines strategic consulting with tools and processes to help nonprofits build a stronger, more sustainable grant-seeking program.

## Features

- Grant prospect research and prioritization
- Proposal strategy and writing support
- Help manage complex and federal grants
- Coaching to strengthen internal grant processes
- Ongoing partnership to grow long-term grant revenue

## Pricing

Pricing is not listed publicly; nonprofits request a consultation to receive a customized quote based on their goals and level of support needed.

## Deployment

Cloud-based service platform.
