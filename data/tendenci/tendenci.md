## Overview

Tendenci is free and open-source association management software designed for nonprofits and associations. It includes membership management, recurring donations, event management, website builder, human resources management, and many more features.

## Key Features

- Membership management
- Recurring donations
- Event management
- Website builder
- Human resources management
- Open source (self-hosted)

## Pricing

Free and open-source. Self-hosted deployment.
