## Overview

GiveHub is a comprehensive donation platform built to boost contributions for non-profits and churches through innovative giving technology.

## Key Features

- Recurring Donations
- Matching Gifts
- CRM Integration
- Grant Management
- Donor Segmentation
- Email Marketing
- Automated Receipts
- Donor Database
- Text Giving
- Mobile Apps
- Free Donation Kiosks

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) platform.
