## Overview

ThankView offers personalized video appeals to help nonprofits engage their audience and increase donation rates.

## Features

- Built-in video editor
- Overlays and banners
- Branded landing pages
- Digital envelopes
- Video requests
- Metrics and reporting

## Pricing

ThankView's pricing isn't available online—to get started, contact their sales team.

## Integrations

ThankView integrates with plenty of fundraising apps and tools, including Givebutter.

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS platform.
