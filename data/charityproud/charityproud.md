## Overview

Charityproud is an online donor management system developed by Donor Engine, tailored for startups, SMBs, mid-market, and enterprise organizations.

## Features

- Membership Management
- Recurring Donations
- Matching Gifts
- Customizable Forms
- Software Integration
- Online Donations
- Automated Receipts
- Email Marketing
