## Overview

Sage Intacct is a cloud financial management solution designed for nonprofits and other organizations. It handles core financial operations by supporting multiple currencies and donor entries simultaneously.

## Features

- Core financial operations
- Multi-currency support
- Donor entry management
- Recurring gifting program
- Inventory Management
- Budgeting And Planning
- Multi Entity
- Fixed Assets
- Audit Trail
- Project Accounting
- Customer Management
- Revenue Recognition

## Pricing

Paid solution. License costs vary based on organization size and requirements.

## Deployment

Cloud-based deployment.
