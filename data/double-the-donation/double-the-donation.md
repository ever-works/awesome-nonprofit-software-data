## Overview

Double the Donation enables nonprofits to streamline and promote corporate donation matching to their donors.

## Features

- Streamlined search field
- Automated emails
- Auto-submission

## Pricing

Double the Donation's pricing starts at $999 per year for eligible small nonprofits.

## Integrations

Double the Donation integrates with most major donation forms and fundraising tools, including Givebutter.

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS platform.
