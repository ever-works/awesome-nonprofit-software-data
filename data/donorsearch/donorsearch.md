## Overview

DonorSearch is an AI-powered tool that provides nonprofits with actionable insights into their donor base.

## Features

- Prospect list building
- Donor engagement
- Marketing plans and lists
- Wealth screening

## Pricing

DonorSearch's products are priced on a per-month basis, but the exact cost isn't publicly available. Get in touch with DonorSearch directly to book a demo or receive a custom quote.

## Integrations

DonorSearch integrates with 40+ CRM systems and fundraising tools, including Bloomerang, Salesforce, and Givebutter.

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS platform.
