## Overview

Flodesk is an email marketing platform that helps nonprofits create eye-catching signup forms, emails, and digital newsletters. Teams can use it to launch list-building forms and automate welcome sequences.

## Key Features

- Email templates
- Email scheduling
- Signup forms
- Landing pages
- Custom fonts
- Segmentation
- Custom fields
- Data analytics

## Pricing

Limited features are available for free. Email marketing plans start at $38 per month.

## Integrations

Flodesk integrates with popular web tools such as Shopify, WordPress, and Slack.

## Use Cases

Fundraisers can build signup forms, automate welcome sequences for new supporters, and create newsletters for email campaigns.
