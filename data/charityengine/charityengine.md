## Overview

CharityEngine is an all-in-one donor management and CRM solution built specifically for nonprofits that want to streamline their fundraising, marketing, and operations.

## Features

- Unified CRM, fundraising, and email marketing platform
- Donation processing and recurring gift management
- Event management and ticketing
- Marketing automation and segmentation
- Payment processing and donor self-service portal
- Advanced reporting and analytics

## Pricing

CharityEngine offers several packages with pricing that varies based on the features you need and your organization's size. Request a demo to receive a customized quote that fits your goals and budget.

## Integrations

- QuickBooks
- Google Analytics
- Mailchimp
- Custom API integrations

Cloud-based SaaS platform.
