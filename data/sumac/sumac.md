## Overview

Sumac is nonprofit software that centralizes membership management, fund management, and volunteer management to save time and effort.

## Features

- Campaign Management
- Software Integration
- Payment Management
- Email Marketing
- Group Management
- Contact Database
- Pledge Management
- Mobile Access

## Pricing

- CRM Lite: $99 per month
- CRM Pro: $199 per month

## Deployment

Cloud-based deployment.
