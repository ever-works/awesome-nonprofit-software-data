## Overview

Consul is free and open-source collaborative decision making and budgeting software used by many government bodies all around the world. It enables citizen participation in decision-making and participatory budgeting processes.

## Key Features

- Participatory budgeting
- Collaborative decision making
- Citizen proposals and voting
- Debate and discussion tools
- Open source (self-hosted)
