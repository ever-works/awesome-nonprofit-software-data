## Overview

ERPNext is an open-source fully featured ERP written in Python. It provides accounting, inventory, project management, CRM, and other business management capabilities that can be used by nonprofit organizations.

## Key Features

- Accounting and financial management
- Inventory management
- Project management
- Customer relationship management
- Open source (self-hosted)
- Python-based
