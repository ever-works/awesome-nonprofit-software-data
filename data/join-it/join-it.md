## Overview

Join It is a simple, modern membership management platform built for nonprofits that want an intuitive, no-frills way to manage members.

## Features

- Custom membership levels and pricing
- Automated renewals and reminders
- Member directory and profiles
- Integrated payment processing
- Event and ticketing connections

## Pricing

Plans start at $29/month, with pricing based on the number of members and automation needs.

## Integrations

- Eventbrite
- Mailchimp
- Constant Contact
- Stripe
- Zapier

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS platform.
