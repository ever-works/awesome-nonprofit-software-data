## Overview

Mattermost is a semi-open-source Slack alternative that provides secure, self-hosted team messaging for organizations. It includes file sharing, search, audio and video calls, and integrations with other tools.

## Key Features

- Secure team messaging
- File sharing and search
- Audio and video calls
- Integrations with third-party tools
- Self-hosted deployment
- Open source core

## Pricing

Free and open-source core. Paid cloud and enterprise plans available.
