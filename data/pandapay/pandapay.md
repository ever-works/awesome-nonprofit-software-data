## Overview

PandaPay is donor management software developed by Alboddo Technology, tailored for startups, SMBs, mid-market, and enterprise organizations.

## Features

- Automated Receipts
- Data Export
- Donor Segmentation
- Event Management
- CRM Integration
- Grant Management
- Donor Database
- Mobile Access
